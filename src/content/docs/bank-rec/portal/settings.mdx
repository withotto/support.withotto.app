We know every practice and client is different, so we want you to be able to use Otto in a way that works for you.

There are two levels of settings:

1. **Practice-wide** The place to set things up that should apply to all (or at least most) of your clients
2. **Client-level** Anything specific to that client

## Reconciliation settings

You can configure Otto's reconciliation settings at a practice-wide level and at the client level. There are similarities but also some differences between the two places.

### Practice-wide

<ZoomableImage
  src={practiceReconciliationSettingsImage}
  alt="The reconciliation settings for a practice"
/>

#### Bank rule reconciliation

Bank rule reconciliations are enabled practice-wide by default. You would be the first to disable it for all your clients, but we won't judge!

#### SmartMatch reconciliation

SmartMatch is set to monitor mode when your trial starts, so you can check Otto's predictions before letting him reconcile green matches for your clients. Some practices are happy with only using bank rule matches, so disable SmartMatch. Maybe we're a _little_ biased, but we think SmartMatch is pretty great so recommend you give it a try.

<Aside type="caution">
  Otto will only perform GuidedMatches if SmartMatch is enabled.
</Aside>

#### Minimum SmartMatch confidence

This is the score Otto must reach before he will reconcile a match. Practice-wide, you can only raise the minimum confidence level. That reduces the chance of incorrect SmartMatch reconciliations but makes Otto less likely to reconcile matches generally.

### Client-specific

<ZoomableImage
  src={clientSettingsImage}
  alt="The reconciliation settings for a client"
/>

These settings are very similar to the practice-wide settings. By default, clients will inherit the same settings that you apply practice-wide. For any clients that should have different settings, simply make the necessary changes. You can see what setting has been specified practice-wide.

#### Minimum SmartMatch confidence

You have a bit more flexibility with this setting for individual clients. The minimum confidence level is 70%. We recommend you watch Otto's scores for each client for a few weeks before lowering this setting to make sure you're not going to end up with extra work due to incorrect reconciliations.

### Bank account filtering

<Aside>
  Bank account filtering is available on request. Open the help launcher in the
  portal, choose **Chat with support**, and we'll enable it for you.
</Aside>

There may be bank accounts for some clients that you want Otto to skip. Perhaps you already have a process for cash coding certain accounts, a member of staff needs to handle specific reconciliations, or you give clients a window to send documents before reconciling as spend money.

Bank account filtering gives you per-account control over what Otto does. For each bank account, you can:

- Uncheck any accounts Otto should completely ignore
- Enable or disable bank rule reconciliations
- Enable or disable SmartMatch reconciliations

Each dropdown has these options:

- **Same as client** follows whatever you've set for the client, which is shown in brackets. If you change the client or practice setting later, accounts set to Same as client will follow the change automatically.
- **Enabled** turns reconciliation on for this account, regardless of the setting above.
- **Monitor** (SmartMatch only) records Otto's SmartMatch decisions for this account without reconciling them, regardless of the setting above.
- **Disabled** turns reconciliation off for this account, regardless of the setting above.

<Aside type="caution">
    Otto only finds out which bank accounts are present when he logs in each day. Any new accounts will be reconciled by
    default and you can change your preferences afterwards.

    Otto will recognise when a bank account is renamed and will be able to tell it is the same underlying account,
    keeping any rules you have already set.

</Aside>

## Scheduling

<ZoomableImage
  src={schedulingSettingsImage}
  alt="The scheduling settings for the practice"
/>

### Practice timezone

You might be thinking: _Huh? Why would I need to set this?_ Glad you asked!

Otto needs to make sure he's working when you expect him to, and your staff needs to know when to expect Otto to have completed the reconciliations. Some practices may have staff in different timezones and everyone needs to be on the same page about when the reconciliations will happen, so we have a default timezone and convert it for anyone who is somewhere else.

### Time

This is the hour of the day that Otto will perform reconciliations for you. Otto can start any time within the period and should finish before the end. We have monitoring in place to alert us if anything stops him from meeting this commitment so we can put fixes in place.

You can also see when Otto is next due to reconcile.

Otto keeps a watchful eye on your reconciliation schedule. If you change your preferred reconciliation time, he'll check each hour to make sure no reconciliations have been missed.

Let's say at 09:45 you change your daily reconciliation time from late evening (22:00–23:00) to early morning (06:00–07:00). When Otto does his hourly check at 10:00, he'll notice this gap and promptly catch up on any missed reconciliations. This ensures your books stay up to date, even when schedules change.

<Aside type="caution" title="Please note">
  Otto will reconcile a maximum of once per day
</Aside>

### Schedule days

By default, Otto works every day of the week. However, you can choose which days Otto should perform reconciliations.

#### Practice-wide schedule

Set a default schedule for your whole practice, and Otto will follow this for all your clients. For example, if you only want Otto to work Monday to Friday, simply select those days in your practice settings.

#### Client-specific schedules

Need different timing for particular clients? You can override the practice schedule with a custom schedule for individual clients. This is useful when clients have specific expectations about when their books should be reconciled or when they typically forward receipts.

<Aside type="tip" title="Why use a custom schedule?">
  Some practices have bank rules set up as a fallback for when clients don't
  provide receipts. Rather than Otto immediately reconciling those bank rules,
  you can configure him to wait a few days, giving your clients time to submit
  their receipts first. Once the receipts are published in Xero, Otto can then
  reconcile them properly.
</Aside>

## Billing settings

### Billing type

Each plan comes with a certain number of included credits. These are used up when Otto reconciles matches in Xero. Your credits for a billing period are your plan's included credits, plus any credits carried over from earlier periods, plus any adjustments. There are two billing types that give you control over your With Otto bill.

#### Uncapped

When your credits run out, Otto keeps reconciling. The extra credits he uses are billed on your next invoice at your plan's per-credit price.

#### Fixed

Ensure you never pay more than you have planned. Otto won't start a reconciliation that would take you past your credits and the extra credits limit you set. He starts again when your next billing period begins.

GuidedMatches and reconciliations in your practice's own Xero are free, so he keeps doing those after reaching the limit.

The extra credits limit lets Otto reconcile beyond your credits. Set it to 0 if you don't want him to use any extra credits. Any extra credits he uses are billed on your next invoice at your plan's per-credit price.

For example, say your plan includes 500 credits, you have 50 credits carried over, and you allow 100 extra credits. Otto won't start a reconciliation that would take you past 650 credits in that billing period. If he uses 620 credits, the 70 extra credits are on your next invoice.

### Billing country

Your billing country is required. It determines which taxes, such as VAT in the UK, we add to your bill.
