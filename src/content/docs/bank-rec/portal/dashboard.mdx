## Recent reconciliations

For each client you will see up to ten rows, for the ten most recent days in the last two months when Otto reconciled something or made a SmartMatch decision. For older days, go to the client's page. Click **View** on a row to open that day's [report](/bank-rec/portal/reconciliation-report/).
