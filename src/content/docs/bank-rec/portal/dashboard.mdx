The dashboard shows what is left for you to check across your clients: the lines Otto reconciled that nobody has given feedback on yet, and the ones where your feedback would help him.

## The two views

- **To check** lists each day with lines left to check, in three groups, newest first. Days with nothing left fold into one line at the bottom.
- **By client** shows each client's days in a table.

The dashboard remembers your view, period and **Show days with nothing to check** switch, so it opens the same way next time.

## What the counts mean

Each day shows how many of its lines are left to check at each level:

- **Reconciled:** Otto reconciled them, and nobody has said yet whether he was right.
- **Would reconcile:** Otto would have reconciled them, but SmartMatch is in monitoring mode.
- **Worth a look:** Otto skipped them, but his pick is likely right: his score was close to the required level, or Xero offered only that one possible match.
- **Skipped:** the other lines Otto skipped.

## When a line is checked

A line stays on the list until someone gives feedback on it. One answer from anyone in your practice clears it for everyone. A line Otto didn't reconcile is also cleared when someone marks one of its possible matches as a match, or when Otto reconciles the transaction on a later run.

Opening a report doesn't clear anything. It only removes the dot that marks days you haven't opened since Otto last added to them. The dot is yours alone: a colleague opening the day doesn't remove it for you.

## A routine that works

Work through **To check** from the top:

1. **Reconciliations without feedback.** These come first, because a wrong reconciliation is a mistake in Xero that you'll need to undo there.
2. **Would reconcile in monitor mode.** These show whether Otto is ready for SmartMatch to be enabled for the client.
3. **Feedback that would help Otto.** Answer these as time allows. Otto left these transactions for you, so nothing in Xero needs fixing if you don't. Each answer helps him learn, and where SmartMatch is enabled, a match lets him reconcile the transaction for you as a free [GuidedMatch](/bank-rec/guides/guidedmatch/).

Choose the period to suit how often you check: **7 days** if you check most days, **30 days** or **60 days** if you check now and then. A day drops off the list once it is older than the period, even with lines left on it, but it stays on the [client's page](/bank-rec/portal/clients/#details).
