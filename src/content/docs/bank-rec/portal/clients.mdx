## The list

The place to go to see all the clients that Otto has seen in your practice and when he last checked them to see if he could reconcile anything.

The table lists the clients assigned to Otto in Xero HQ. For each one it shows its **Status**, whether bank rules and SmartMatch are on, Otto's **Xero role**, and when he last checked it. Clients Otto no longer has access to are listed under **Unassigned clients** below the table. That section is collapsed until you open it.

To give Otto more clients, click **Add new clients**. See [giving Otto access to your clients](/bank-rec/getting-started/giving-otto-access-to-your-clients/) for the steps.

## Details

This is where you can see everything Otto knows about your client.

The top section is a summary of what Otto has done, the settings that are applied, when Otto last checked that client, and when he was last trained.

Underneath is a table showing _every. single. reconciliation. ever._ (for that client at least.) We think it's important to see a full history of what Otto has done in each of your clients accounts so you can check back to see what happened, when, and why.

<ZoomableImage src={clientDetailsImage} alt="The client details page" />

## Manual reconciliations

Sometimes it might be helpful if Otto could reconcile a client outside your daily reconciliation schedule. Maybe you've just added a new client, created a bunch of new bank rules, or imported some bank transactions. Whatever the reason, you can call Otto to reconcile a client manually by clicking **Reconcile now** on the client details page.
