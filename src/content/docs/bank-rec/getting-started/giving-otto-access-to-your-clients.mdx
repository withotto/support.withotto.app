Otto is like another employee (except he doesn't need a computer, a branded notepad, or annual leave) so accesses Xero using a web browser. His access is determined by you by inviting him to Xero HQ and assigning clients to him. You can add or remove clients at any time.

### Assigning your practice Xero account

<Aside type="caution">
  Your practice transactions (salaries, fees, etc.) will be visible to staff who
  have access to all clients. To maintain privacy, ensure staff are assigned
  only to their relevant clients before adding your practice account.
</Aside>

It's not possible to assign users access to a practice Xero account via Xero HQ. Instead, invite Otto from your practice's own Xero organisation:

1. Log in to your practice Xero organisation
2. Click your organisation name on the top left of the page
3. Select **Settings**, then **Users**
4. Click **Invite a user**
5. Enter the email address shown on the [Add new clients](https://portal.withotto.app/dashboard/clients/add/) page in the portal, and the name "With Otto".
6. Check **Business and accounting** and make sure the other sections are unchecked
7. Select **Standard** and uncheck any checkboxes in the **Business and accounting** section
8. Click **Send invite**

### Assigning clients where you don't have manage users access

<Aside>
  When your client hasn't granted you manage users access, you'll need to ask
  them to invite Otto.
</Aside>

Your clients can invite Otto from their Xero organisation. We will need to accept the invitation before Otto will have access.

Here are the steps for your client to follow:

1. Log in to your Xero organisation
2. Click your organisation name on the top left of the page
3. Select **Settings**, then **Users**
4. Click **Invite a user**
5. Enter your accountant or bookkeeper's Otto email address, and the name "With Otto"
6. Check **Business and accounting** and make sure the other sections are unchecked
7. Select **Standard** and uncheck any checkboxes in the **Business and accounting** section
8. Click **Send invite**

Otto's email address is shown on the [Add new clients](https://portal.withotto.app/dashboard/clients/add/) page in the portal. Choose "A client's organisation, and I don't have Manage users access" there, and click **Copy steps for your client** to copy these steps with the address filled in, ready to send to your client.

## Updating your client list in the portal

The client list in the portal updates each time Otto reconciles your practice, so clients you assign to him appear after his next scheduled reconciliation. To see them sooner:

1. Go to the [clients list](https://portal.withotto.app/dashboard/clients/) in the portal and click **Add new clients**
2. Once you've finished the steps in Xero, click **Update client list**
3. Wait for the update to finish. It should take no more than five minutes, and you don't need to stay on the page.
4. Refresh the clients list to see the new clients

New clients have a "New" badge for 48 hours.

The Add new clients page also shows the steps for each way of giving Otto access, depending on whose organisation it is and whether you have Manage users access to it.

## Checking Otto has the correct access

Like Goldilocks and her porridge, it is important that Otto has neither too much nor too little access to your clients. You can check he has just the right role by viewing the [clients list](https://portal.withotto.app/dashboard/clients/) in the portal. The **Xero role** column shows Otto's role for each client:

- A red padlock means Otto has too little access to reconcile that client.
- An amber triangle means Otto has more access than he needs. For most clients, Standard (No Reports) is enough. For a client on a Ledger or Cashbook plan, he needs Adviser.
