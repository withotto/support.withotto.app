Otto doesn't know your clients as well as you do (and he's naturally very cautious), so it's likely he won't be confident enough to reconcile all the transactions he could, particularly at the start of his time with you.

Our top priority is to avoid Otto making incorrect reconciliations so, by default, we require him to be very confident before making a reconciliation. Nonetheless, he may still incorrectly reconcile something once in a while.

Feedback is how you can help him work out what he should and should not reconcile.

## What happens when you give feedback

The feedback will always be used to make Otto more accurate in the future, but some other things can happen depending on the action he took and the type of feedback you give.

#### Otto reconciled; you agree it's a match

Perfect, just what we're aiming for! Nothing extra happens in this case. We'll let Otto know he was correct when he is next retrained.

#### Otto reconciled, but it was not correct

Sorry. We do what we can to avoid this, but Otto isn't infallible. Here's what happens next:

1. Tell Otto he made a mistake by clicking the "This is not a match" button in the portal
   - This will make sure he doesn't try to reconcile the same transaction again
2. You'll need to undo the mistake he made in Xero.

We don't want to make the situation worse, so Otto now won't touch that bank statement transaction, even if another bill or invoice becomes available in the future. You will need to reconcile the match yourself in Xero.

<Aside>
  An incorrect SmartMatch you report isn't charged if Otto made it at 90%
  confidence or above. We only want you to pay when Otto is doing what you want
  him to. If you lowered the required confidence and he reconciled a match below
  90%, it stays charged whatever feedback you give.
</Aside>

#### Otto skipped; you agree it's not a match

Thanks for confirming. Otto will learn from it when he is next retrained.

He also won't touch that bank statement transaction again, even if another bill or invoice becomes available later. When the right match is there, reconcile it yourself in Xero.

If Xero offered [several possible matches](/bank-rec/guides/feedback/#when-xero-offers-several-possible-matches) and the right one is in the list, mark it as a match there instead, and Otto will reconcile it for you.

#### Otto skipped, but he should have reconciled

There will always be a few tricky matches that Otto might get tripped up by, but your feedback will help him improve over time.

Let Otto know that he should have reconciled the bank transaction and the bill or invoice by clicking the "This is a match" button. If Xero offered several possible matches and a different one is right, mark that one in the [list of possible matches](/bank-rec/guides/feedback/#when-xero-offers-several-possible-matches) instead.

This does two things:

1. The next time Otto reconciles that client, he'll see that you said he should have reconciled this transaction and will perform a [GuidedMatch](/bank-rec/guides/guidedmatch/). He only does this where SmartMatch is enabled for the bank account, not in monitoring mode.
2. The feedback will be used to improve Otto's decisions when he is next retrained

<Aside>There is no charge for GuidedMatch reconciliations.</Aside>

## When Xero offers several possible matches

When Xero offers more than one possible match for a bank transaction, Otto leaves it for you to check. Under the card, open "Xero offered … possible matches for this line" to see them all. The list shows each one with Otto's score for it, and highlights the one the card shows.

<ZoomableImage
  src={possibleMatchesImage}
  alt="A SmartMatch card Otto left for you, with its list of three possible matches open: three bills from the same supplier for the same amount, each with Otto's score and Match and Not a match buttons"
  label="Possible matches"
/>

Each possible match has its own buttons:

- **Match:** where SmartMatch is enabled, Otto reconciles that one on his next run as a free [GuidedMatch](/bank-rec/guides/guidedmatch/). A transaction has only one right match, so marking another one moves your "Match" to it. This works even if you've marked something on the line as not a match.
- **Not a match, on the one the card shows:** this is the same as clicking "This is not a match" on the card. Otto leaves the transaction for you from then on.
- **Not a match, on any of the others:** this rules out that one only. Otto won't reconcile it, and you can still mark a different one as a match.
