Otto Capture connects to QuickBooks® Online so you can publish bills and supplier credits straight into your client's QuickBooks Online company. This page covers the QuickBooks Online specifics. For the parts that work the same on every platform, see [Connecting your accounting platform](/capture/platforms/connecting-your-accounting-platform/).

## Connecting a client

Each client in Capture connects to a single QuickBooks Online company. There's no practice-level connection, so you connect each client's company separately. The person who connects it needs a QuickBooks Online user role that can create bills.

<Steps>
    1. From the **Clients** page in Capture, click **Add client**, then choose **QuickBooks Online**

    2. Sign in to QuickBooks Online and choose the company you want to connect

    3. Check the company named on the authorisation screen, then click **Connect**

    4. You'll be redirected back to Capture, and Capture starts syncing straight away. Complete setup for the client to start publishing

</Steps>

## Permissions

Capture asks for access to the company's accounting data. It uses that access to read the reference data listed below, create suppliers, create bills and supplier credits, and attach the original documents to them. It also adds a currency to the company when you choose to add one during review. It doesn't ask to sign you in to Capture with your Intuit account.

## Synced data

| Data                  | Purpose                                                                                                           |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Suppliers**         | Match the supplier on each document                                                                               |
| **Customers**         | Not used on documents. Synced so Capture knows which names are already taken (see [Contacts](#contacts))          |
| **Chart of accounts** | Fill the **Category** options on each line. System accounts such as accounts payable and VAT control are left out |
| **VAT codes**         | Apply the correct VAT treatment to each line                                                                      |
| **Currencies**        | Set the document currency. With multi-currency off, only the home currency is available                           |
| **Classes**           | Assign a class to each line, where the company tracks classes on each line (see [Classes](#classes))              |
| **Company details**   | Home currency, whether the company charges VAT, and its country                                                   |

## Publishing

Otto publishes purchases to QuickBooks Online:

- **Bills:** he creates these as QuickBooks Online bills
- **Purchase credit notes:** he creates these as supplier credits, which is the name you'll see in the document type picker for these clients

Capture doesn't publish sales invoices or sales credit notes to QuickBooks Online, so they're marked **Triage failed**. If Otto got the type wrong, change it on the review screen. QuickBooks Online has no publish state to choose.

## VAT

Otto sends the VAT figures from the document, so QuickBooks Online records the VAT the supplier actually charged. Where QuickBooks Online would work out the VAT itself and reach a different figure, Otto doesn't publish, and the review screen says which line to fix and how.

## Currencies

With multi-currency turned on, you can publish in the document's currency, adding it to the company during review if needed. With it off, convert the amounts to the home currency first (see [Currency and conversion](/capture/guides/reviewing/#currency-and-conversion)).

QuickBooks Online uses the exchange rate from the company's own rate table for the document's issue date. A supplier's currency can't be changed once it's set, so add a supplier set up in a different currency again under a different name.

## Classes

Capture syncs classes only where the company tracks classes on each line. If it tracks them on the whole transaction instead, no classes sync and the review screen doesn't offer a class selector.

## Contacts

QuickBooks Online keeps suppliers and customers as separate lists, and Otto publishes to suppliers only. A name can only be used once across suppliers, customers, and employees in QuickBooks Online. If you create a supplier during review whose name is already taken, Capture asks for a different **Name in QuickBooks**. The contact keeps the name on the document in Capture, so Otto still recognises it.

## Attachments

Otto attaches the original document to both bills and supplier credits, up to 20 MB per file, in PDF, JPEG, PNG, GIF, TIFF, DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, or ODS format. He attaches a document in another format, such as a HEIC photo or an HTML email, as the PDF or image Capture made from it.

## Disconnecting and reconnecting

Disconnecting in Capture revokes Otto's access in QuickBooks Online too. You can also remove Otto Capture from inside QuickBooks Online: Capture checks each connection once a day and moves the client to **Disconnected**. For what disconnecting deletes and how to reconnect, see [Disconnecting and reconnecting](/capture/platforms/connecting-your-accounting-platform/#disconnecting-and-reconnecting).
