Otto Capture publishes finished bills and invoices to your client's Xero, FreeAgent, or QuickBooks Online organisation, and each client connects to one organisation. This page covers what works the same on every platform. For the authorisation steps, permissions, and what syncs, see the [Xero](/capture/platforms/xero/), [FreeAgent](/capture/platforms/freeagent/), or [QuickBooks Online](/capture/platforms/quickbooks/) page.

## Adding a client

<Steps>
    1. From the **Clients** page in Capture, click **Add client**

    2. Choose the accounting platform you want to connect

    3. Authorise Capture on the platform's sign-in screen

    4. You'll be redirected back to Capture, and data sync begins automatically

</Steps>

Capture then syncs the client's contacts, accounts or categories, tax rates, currencies, and organisation details, which Otto uses when he suggests the coding.

## Connection status

Each client shows one of four connection statuses on the **Clients** page and on the client settings page:

| Status                | Meaning                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Active**            | Connected and syncing. Otto can publish bills and invoices.                                              |
| **Pending setup**     | Connected, but setup isn't finished. Finish setup before submitting documents.                           |
| **Disconnected**      | The connection has been removed, either by you in Capture or, on some platforms, on the platform's side. |
| **Connection failed** | Capture tried to sync or publish and the platform rejected the connection. Reconnect to resume.          |

## Disconnecting and reconnecting

You can disconnect a client at any time from the client settings. Capture shows what will be deleted and what stays before you confirm. In short:

- Syncing and publishing stop, and the client's [submission page](/capture/getting-started/client-submission-page/) stops working. Documents sent by email and WhatsApp are still accepted
- The data synced from the platform is deleted, along with the supplier matches Otto has learned for the client. Once you reconnect, he no longer uses the bills published before to suggest coding
- Documents in Capture, and anything already published to the platform, stay

To reconnect, open the client settings and click **Reconnect**. Capture syncs the platform's data again and Otto resumes publishing, learning the client's suppliers and coding again as you publish.

The same data is deleted when access is revoked from inside the platform. A connection that ends without anyone disconnecting it, such as when your subscription lapses, keeps its data and learning, and an organisation admin can remove it with **Delete synced data**. Whether a revocation inside the platform reaches Capture depends on the platform: see its page.

### When Capture ends a Xero connection

Xero recommends that connected apps don't hold on to connections they no longer use, so Capture disconnects a Xero client after 97 days with no activity: nothing published and no data refreshed. Organisation admins get a notification at 90 days. To keep the connection, publish a document for the client, or click **Refresh all** on the client's **Accounting platform** tab. A client disconnected this way keeps its synced data and coding history, and you reconnect it in the usual way.
