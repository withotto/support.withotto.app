Each client in Capture connects to a single Xero organisation. This page covers the Xero-specific parts of connecting and publishing. For the parts that work the same on every platform, see [Connecting your accounting platform](/capture/platforms/connecting-your-accounting-platform/).

## Connecting a client

The Xero user who authorises the connection needs the Standard or Adviser role in that organisation.

<Steps>
    1. Navigate to the **Clients** page in Capture and click **Add client**, then choose **Xero**

    2. You'll be redirected to Xero's authorisation screen, and may need to log in

    3. Select the organisation you want to connect and click **Allow access**

    4. You'll be redirected back to Capture, and Capture starts syncing straight away. Complete setup for the client to start publishing

</Steps>

## Signing up through the Xero App Store

If you install Otto Capture from the Xero App Store, Capture signs you in with your Xero login and prefills what it can when you sign up.

## Permissions

Capture requests access to:

- **Contacts:** To match suppliers and customers on your documents, and create new contacts that are not yet in Xero
- **Accounting:** To read your chart of accounts, tax rates, and currencies; to create bills, invoices, and credit notes; and to add a currency when you choose to add one during review
- **Files:** To attach original documents to bills, invoices, and credit notes

Capture does not request access to data outside of what's needed for document processing.

## Synced data

Once connected, Capture syncs the following data from Xero:

| Data                     | Purpose                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Contacts**             | Match suppliers and customers to extracted document data                                                                                |
| **Chart of accounts**    | Assign account codes to line items, and recognise the client's bank accounts in a [Missing receipts](/capture/missing-receipts/) import |
| **Tax rates**            | Apply the correct tax treatment to line items                                                                                           |
| **Currencies**           | Set the document currency                                                                                                               |
| **Tracking categories**  | Assign tracking categories to line items                                                                                                |
| **Organisation details** | Determine base currency, multi-currency support, tax registration status, and other details needed to process documents correctly       |

## Publishing

### Publish states

When Otto publishes a bill or sales invoice to Xero, he creates it in one of three states. You choose the default for each client when you complete its setup, and can change it later in the client's settings (see [Configuring clients](/capture/getting-started/configuring-clients/#default-publish-state)). You can also override it for a single document during review.

| State                 | Description               | When to use                                                                           |
| --------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Draft**             | Saved as a draft          | When you want to review bills in Xero before they enter the approval process          |
| **Awaiting approval** | Waiting to be approved    | When you use an approval workflow and want bills to go straight to the approval queue |
| **Awaiting payment**  | Approved and ready to pay | When you don't use an approval workflow and want bills ready for payment immediately  |

Sales invoices use the same three states: **Draft** holds the invoice for review, **Awaiting approval** routes it through an approval step, and **Awaiting payment** makes it ready to send and collect on.

### Credit notes

Otto publishes credit notes to Xero as Xero credit notes: a purchase credit note as a credit against accounts payable, and a sales credit note as a credit against accounts receivable. They use the same three publish states as bills and invoices, and Otto attaches the original document in the same way.

## Currencies

Multi-currency isn't part of every Xero plan (see [Xero's pricing plans](https://www.xero.com/uk/pricing-plans/)). Capture asks Xero whether the organisation can use more than one currency, and the review screen offers only what it can do: publishing in the document's currency, adding that currency to the organisation if it isn't enabled yet, or converting the amounts. A currency you add takes effect in Xero straight away and stays available to the organisation. See [Currency and conversion](/capture/guides/reviewing/#currency-and-conversion).

Even where the plan allows it, converting can suit better. A card payment taken in sterling reaches the statement as one sterling figure, so recording the document at the rate actually charged means it agrees with the bank line. Some practices also prefer to keep an occasional foreign receipt out of a single-currency ledger, avoiding revaluations and exchange differences.

## Attachments

Otto attaches the original document file to bills, sales invoices, and credit notes in Xero, up to 10 MB. For a HEIC, HEIF, WebP, or AVIF photo, he attaches the JPEG Capture made from it instead of the original.

Capture also adds a link to each bill and sales invoice it publishes, back to the document in Capture. Xero credit notes have no field for the link. It opens in Capture, so only people who can sign in to your organisation can follow it.

## Missing receipts

You can import the statement lines Xero hasn't matched yet and ask the client about the payments you have no paperwork for. You export the **Uncoded statement lines** report from Xero as a CSV and upload it on the client's **Missing receipts** tab. Capture doesn't read bank transactions through the connection, and needs no extra access in Xero for this. See [Importing from Xero](/capture/missing-receipts/xero/).

## Disconnecting and reconnecting

Three things are specific to Xero:

- **Disconnecting works from either side.** You can disconnect a client in Capture, or revoke Capture's access from inside Xero.
- **Capture picks up a revocation inside Xero.** It checks each client once a day and moves it to **Disconnected**. A sync or publish that reaches the connection before then shows **Connection failed** instead.
- **Capture ends quiet connections, as Xero recommends.** A client with nothing published or refreshed for 97 days is disconnected, after a warning to organisation admins at 90 days. See [When Capture ends a Xero connection](/capture/platforms/connecting-your-accounting-platform/#when-capture-ends-a-xero-connection).

Organisation admins get a notification with a reconnect link. For what disconnecting deletes and how to reconnect, see [Disconnecting and reconnecting](/capture/platforms/connecting-your-accounting-platform/#disconnecting-and-reconnecting).
