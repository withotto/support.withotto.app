---
title: How Capture processes documents
description: The stages a document passes through before it reaches your review queue, and what Otto extracts
---

When you submit a document to Capture, it passes through a few stages before landing in your review queue. Most documents are ready within a minute, and nearly all within a few minutes.

## Pipeline stages

### 1. Triage

Otto checks the document is a genuine financial document and works out which type it is: a bill or receipt, purchase credit note, sales invoice, or sales credit note.

- If he can't tell the type, the document is marked **Needs classification** for you to set it.
- If the file holds more than one document, it's marked **Needs manual split**. Split the file and send each one, or, if the pages all belong to one bill (such as an invoice with the supplier's own expenses attached), click **Process as one document** on the review screen.
- On QuickBooks Online, Otto publishes bills and purchase credit notes only, so a sales invoice or sales credit note is marked **Triage failed**. If he got the type wrong, change it on the review screen (see [QuickBooks Online](/capture/platforms/quickbooks/#publishing)).

### 2. Extraction

Otto reads the document and extracts the fields listed [below](#what-otto-extracts). This is where he does most of his thinking. He suggests an account code and tax rate for each line from the client's synced data, and on a bill from a supplier whose bills you've published for this client before, he draws on how you coded them, so his suggestions get sharper over time. Whether he records VAT depends on the client's [VAT registration](/capture/getting-started/configuring-clients/#synced-accounting-data). On a receipt that prints only VAT-inclusive prices, it also depends on your organisation's [VAT on receipts](/capture/guides/organisation-settings/#vat-on-receipts) setting.

The review screen shows Otto's reasoning for each field, so you can see where he found a value and why he chose it.

### 3. Validation

Capture checks that the line items add up to the document's totals. A mismatch is flagged for you to check during review, but doesn't stop the document reaching your queue.

### 4. Contact matching

Otto matches the supplier or customer against the client's contacts, allowing for variations such as Amazon, Amazon.co.uk, and Amazon.com. Where you've published documents for this client under the same name before, he picks the contact you chose then, and the review screen shows **Auto-matched from your previous picks**.

Once these stages are done, the document is ready for you to [review](/capture/guides/reviewing/). For what each status means, see [document statuses](/capture/getting-started/submitting-documents/#document-statuses).

## What Otto extracts

| Field                      | Description                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Supplier/customer name** | The name of the company or individual on the document                                                                                                    |
| **Invoice number**         | The invoice or receipt reference number                                                                                                                  |
| **Reference**              | A second reference for the transaction, such as a purchase order or job number, where the document has one. Shown for Xero and QuickBooks Online clients |
| **Issue date**             | When the document was issued                                                                                                                             |
| **Due date**               | When payment is due                                                                                                                                      |
| **Currency**               | The currency code (e.g., GBP, USD, EUR)                                                                                                                  |
| **Net amount**             | Total before tax                                                                                                                                         |
| **Tax amount**             | Total tax                                                                                                                                                |
| **Total amount**           | Grand total including tax                                                                                                                                |
| **Line items**             | Individual items with description, quantity, unit amount, line amount, tax rate, and account code (or nominal code, depending on your platform)          |
