This page shows the data that moves between Otto Capture and your client's accounting platform, and what Capture doesn't change. The exact data, and the names each platform uses, differ by platform: see the [Xero](/capture/platforms/xero/), [FreeAgent](/capture/platforms/freeagent/), and [QuickBooks Online](/capture/platforms/quickbooks/) pages.

## What Capture reads

Capture reads the client's contacts, accounts or categories, tax rates, currencies, and organisation details such as base currency and tax registration, plus tracking categories on Xero and classes on QuickBooks Online. It reads them when you connect the client and whenever you refresh them, and uses them only to suggest and check values during extraction and review.

Opening the currency converter fetches a published exchange rate for the document's issue date from an external rates service. Only the two currency codes and the date are sent: no client, document, or organisation data.

For [Missing receipts](/capture/missing-receipts/), Capture reads bank transactions differently on each platform:

- **Xero:** only from a file you export from Xero and upload. Capture refreshes the client's bank accounts from Xero to check the file belongs to that client.
- **FreeAgent:** through the connection, only when you click **Fetch transactions**, and only for the date range you choose. See [Importing from FreeAgent](/capture/missing-receipts/freeagent/).

## What Capture publishes

When you publish a document, Capture creates a bill, sales invoice, or credit note with its contact, creating the contact first if you added one during review, and attaches the original document where the platform supports it.

## What Capture does NOT modify

Capture only reads reference data and creates new documents, contacts, and attachments. It does **not**:

- Modify or delete existing bills, invoices, or contacts
- Access payroll or employee data
- Change your accounts or categories, tax rates, or settings. The one exception: on Xero and QuickBooks Online, it adds a currency to the client's organisation if you choose to add one while reviewing a document
- Access reports or financial statements
- Delete any data
