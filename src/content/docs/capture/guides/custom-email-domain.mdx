By default, each client receives documents at an address like `\{prefix\}@\{your-organisation\}.ottocapture.email`. With a custom domain, clients send documents to an address on your own domain instead, such as `acme@docs.smithaccounting.com`, so they can see it's you they're sending to. The `ottocapture.email` address keeps working alongside it.

## Setting up a custom domain

<Aside type="caution">
  Use a subdomain you keep for Capture, such as `docs.smithaccounting.com`. The
  MX record sends all mail for the domain to Capture, so pointing it at the
  domain your practice uses for email would stop that email reaching you.
</Aside>

<Steps>
    1. Go to **Settings → Email** and enter your custom domain

    2. Add the DNS records the page shows to your domain, or pass them to whoever manages your DNS

    3. Return to the email settings page. Capture checks the records while the page is open, or you can click **Check now**. Once both required records are found, the domain is active

</Steps>

DNS changes usually take effect within minutes, but can take up to 48 hours.

## DNS records

The email settings page gives the exact values, with a button to copy each one.

| Record                 | Required    | What it does                                                          |
| ---------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **MX**                 | Yes         | Sends mail for your domain to Capture                                 |
| **TXT** (`_amazonses`) | Yes         | Proves you own the domain                                             |
| **SPF** (TXT)          | Recommended | Says no server may send mail from the domain, since it only receives  |
| **DMARC** (TXT)        | Recommended | Tells other servers to reject mail pretending to come from the domain |

The two recommended records stop anyone sending email that appears to come from your Capture domain.
