After Otto processes a document, it lands in your review queue. This guide covers checking what he extracted, converting currencies, and publishing to your accounting platform.

## The review interface

The review screen shows the original document beside the data Otto extracted: the contact he matched, the header fields, and the line items with their account codes and tax rates. On Xero, it also shows the state the document will be published in.

<ZoomableImage
  src={reviewInterface}
  alt="The review screen, with the original document on one side and the extracted fields on the other: supplier, dates, amounts, and the line items with their nominal codes and tax rates. Above them, arrows and the position in the queue, reading 5 of 6"
  label="Review screen"
/>

If the sender added a note, such as a WhatsApp caption or a note on the [client missing receipts page](/capture/missing-receipts/client-page/), it appears under the filename as **Note from sender**, and Otto reads it with the document. If the client changes the note after that, the screen says so. Check the coding against the current note.

## Editing extracted data

You can edit every field before publishing, and changes save as you go. The document's edit history records who changed what.

If Otto matched the wrong contact, choose another from the client's synced contacts, or create a new one if the supplier isn't there yet. A new contact is created in the accounting platform when you publish the document.

On Xero, you can also change the publish state for this document (see [Xero publish states](/capture/platforms/xero/#publish-states)). To change it for future documents, update the default in the [client configuration](/capture/getting-started/configuring-clients/) instead.

## Currency and conversion

Otto sets the **Currency** field from the document. You can restate every amount in another currency without leaving the review screen: when the client's platform can't record the document's currency, or when you'd rather record it at the rate the bank actually charged, so it agrees with the bank line.

If the platform can't use the currency, a warning under the **Currency** field says so and offers what's possible: adding the currency, where the platform and plan allow it, or converting the amounts. See [Xero currencies](/capture/platforms/xero/#currencies) and [QuickBooks Online currencies](/capture/platforms/quickbooks/#currencies). FreeAgent's list of currencies is fixed, so there converting is the only option.

### Converting the amounts

Otto doesn't set the exchange rate. You enter the total actually charged, from the bank or card statement, and he works out the rate from it.

<Steps>

1. Set **Currency** to the currency you want the document published in

2. Click **Convert currency** under the **Currency** field

3. Enter the total charged in that currency

4. Check the preview, then click **Apply conversion**

</Steps>

<ZoomableImage
  src={currencyConverter}
  alt="The convert currency dialog, showing the total charged in GBP, the effective rate, and a per-line preview of the converted amounts"
  label="Convert currency dialog"
  caption="Enter the total you were charged. The market rate for the issue date is shown as a reference, and you can see what each line will become before anything changes."
/>

Converted prices are rounded to the penny, and Otto moves any difference onto line amounts, never onto tax. The preview marks each line he adjusted.

To correct a conversion, click **Change conversion**. It starts again from the original amounts, so a second run doesn't compound the first. **Restore original amounts** undoes the conversion completely.

### Checking the total against the market rate

To help catch a mistyped total, the converter shows the market rate for the issue date. If your total is well away from it, Capture asks you to check before applying. Card fees and a late payment can both explain a gap, so if the total matches the statement, click **Apply anyway**.

## Publishing

When you're happy with the data, click **Publish**. Capture checks the document first and lists anything you need to fix. Some checks are only warnings, and you can publish past them.

Capture creates the bill or invoice in your accounting platform, creating its contact first if you added one during review, and attaches the original document. Sales invoices keep the file only on Xero, because FreeAgent's have no attachment field (see attachments on [Xero](/capture/platforms/xero/#attachments), [FreeAgent](/capture/platforms/freeagent/#attachments) and [QuickBooks Online](/capture/platforms/quickbooks/#attachments)). On Xero, the document is created in the publish state you chose. FreeAgent and QuickBooks Online have no state to set.

### Other copies of a document

The same document sometimes reaches Capture twice, such as an invoice and a receipt for one purchase. Capture treats another document as a copy when it's for the same client, has the same document type, contact and invoice number, and hasn't been discarded. The review screen shows any copies in a note.

<ZoomableImage
  src={copiesNote}
  alt="The note on the review screen headed This may already be in Xero, linking a published copy of the receipt and offering Discard this document"
  label="Note about a copy"
/>

Publishing a document that has a copy asks you to confirm first, and the document's timeline records the copies you were shown.

This is separate from **Duplicate detected**, which catches the same file sent in twice before Otto reads it.

## Handling publish errors

If publishing fails, the document shows **Publish failed** with the error. Most often, an account code or tax rate has changed in the accounting platform since Capture last synced it. Refresh the client's synced data, update the line, then publish again.

## Discarding documents

If a document shouldn't be published, click **Discard** on the review screen. On a document you're still reviewing, it's in the **More actions** menu beside **Publish**. Choose a reason, and Capture discards the document.

<ZoomableImage
  src={discardReasons}
  alt="The list Capture shows after Discard is clicked, headed Why discard this document?, with six reasons and a short hint under four of them, and Cancel at the foot"
  label="Discard reasons"
/>

The reason shows under the contact name on the **Discarded** tab. To restore a document, open it from that tab and click **Restore**.

<ZoomableImage
  src={discardedTab}
  alt="A client's Discarded tab, with a discarded receipt from Millstone Flour Supplies showing Unreadable or incomplete under the contact name"

/>

To discard several documents at once, select them on the **Needs attention** or **Ready** tab and click **Discard selected**.
