Organisation settings control how your Capture organisation appears, who has access, and how email is configured. Open them from **Settings** in the navigation.

## Organisation prefix

The organisation prefix is part of every client's default email address and [client submission page](/capture/getting-started/client-submission-page/) address:

```
{prefix}@{organisation-prefix}.ottocapture.email
{organisation-prefix}.capture.withotto.app/{prefix}/{code}
```

<Aside type="caution">
  If you change your organisation prefix, the previous email addresses and
  submission page links stop working, and there's no redirect from the old ones.
  Update any email forwarding rules that use the old addresses, and share the
  new link with anyone using a client submission page.
</Aside>

## Team

Invite team members from **Settings → Team** by entering their email address. An invitation expires if it isn't accepted.

| Role       | Description                                                                               |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Admin**  | Full access to all settings, clients, and team management                                 |
| **Member** | Can review and publish documents, but cannot manage organisation settings or team members |

An admin can turn a team member's access off on the same page. It takes effect straight away, and the person stays on the list marked **No access**, so you keep a record of who has had access. Turn it back on from their row at any time. It doesn't affect any other organisations they belong to.

## Billing

Admins can see the organisation's billing from **Settings → Billing**, including each client's tier for the current period and past invoices. Click **Manage billing** to update your payment method and billing address in Stripe.

Capture sets the VAT on each invoice from the billing address recorded in Stripe. Organisations in the UK and the Isle of Man are charged VAT at 20%. Organisations elsewhere aren't charged VAT, and the reverse charge applies. See [Billing](/capture/faq/#billing) in the FAQ for how pricing works.
