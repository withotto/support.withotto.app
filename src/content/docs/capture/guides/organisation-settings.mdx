Organisation settings control how your Capture organisation appears, who has access, and how email is configured. Access these from **Settings** in the navigation.

## General

### Organisation name

Your organisation name is displayed throughout the Capture interface.

### Organisation slug

The slug is used in your default email addresses:

```
{prefix}@{slug}.ottocapture.email
```

It's also the first part of the web address of every [client submission page](/capture/getting-started/client-submission-page):

```
{slug}.capture.withotto.app/{prefix}/{code}
```

Changing the slug updates all default email addresses and all client submission page addresses.

<Aside type="caution">
  If you change your slug, the previous email addresses and submission page
  links stop working, and there's no redirect from the old ones. Update any
  email forwarding rules that use the old addresses, and share the new link with
  anyone using a client submission page.
</Aside>

## Team

You can invite team members to your organisation and manage their access from **Settings → Team**.

### Roles

| Role       | Description                                                                               |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Admin**  | Full access to all settings, clients, and team management                                 |
| **Member** | Can review and publish documents, but cannot manage organisation settings or team members |

### Inviting team members

Invite new team members by entering their email address from the **Settings → Team** page. Invitations expire if not accepted.

### Turning a team member's access off and on

Admins can turn a team member's access off at any time from the **Settings → Team** page. Click **Turn off access** on their row, then **Confirm?**. It takes effect straight away: they can no longer open anything in your organisation, and see an **Access turned off** page if they try.

The person stays on the team list with a **No access** label rather than being deleted, so you keep a record of who has had access. To restore it, click **Turn on access** on their row. Turning off someone's access here doesn't affect any other organisations they belong to.

## Email

You can configure a custom email domain for your organisation. This lets clients receive documents at addresses on your own domain instead of the default `ottocapture.email` addresses.

See [Custom email domain](/capture/guides/custom-email-domain) for setup instructions.
