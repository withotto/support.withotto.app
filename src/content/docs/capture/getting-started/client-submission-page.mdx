Each client has its own submission page: a link you share with whoever sends their receipts and invoices. They open it, take a photo or choose files, and the documents arrive in that client's review queue. There's nothing to download and no account to create. It suits clients who won't set up email forwarding or would rather not use WhatsApp for work.

## How it works

<Steps>

1. You share the client's submission link with whoever sends in their receipts and invoices.

2. They open it on a phone or computer, and take a photo or pick files they already have.

3. The documents go straight into that client's [review queue](/capture/guides/reviewing/).

</Steps>

## Sharing the link

Open the client's settings and go to **Client submission**. The **Client submission page** area holds the link. Copy the link on its own, or a ready-to-send message with the link in it.

## What the person sending documents sees

They can send several files at once, and the same link works every time they have more. Each file can be up to 25 MB (see [supported file types](/capture/getting-started/#supported-file-types)).

  There's no way to add a note to a document sent this way. If context matters
  for a particular document, ask the sender to send it by email or over

</Aside>

## Treat the link like a key

Anyone holding the link can send documents to that client, and nothing else is asked of them. Share it directly with the people who need it, rather than posting it where several people can see. Search engines are told not to index the page.

Unlike a WhatsApp invite, the link is used every time a document is sent, so there's no way to cut off one person. Rotating it stops it working for everyone.

## Rotating and turning the page off

Both actions are admin-only.

- **Rotate link** replaces the link straight away. Everyone who should still send documents needs the new one.
- **Turn off client submission page** switches the route off for that client. Turning it back on later creates a fresh link.

Documents already submitted are unaffected either way, and an old link never comes back.

## What breaks a link, and what doesn't

| Change                                                                                               | Effect on the link                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Changing the client's name or prefix                                                                 | Keeps working. The address updates itself when the page is next opened               |
| Changing your organisation prefix in [Organisation settings](/capture/guides/organisation-settings/) | Breaks every client's link, with no redirect. Share the new ones                     |
| Disconnecting the client's accounting platform                                                       | Stops working. The same link resumes once the client is reconnected and set up again |
| Rotating the link, or turning the page off                                                           | Breaks the old one immediately                                                       |

## Spam protection

To stop the page being used to flood your review queue, each link has a limit set well above normal use. If a client hits it while sending a reasonable number of documents, please contact support.
