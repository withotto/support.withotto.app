Clients can send receipts and invoices to Capture over WhatsApp. It suits the people who actually hold the receipts: there's nothing for them to download and no account to create.

## How it works

<Steps>

1. You share a client's WhatsApp invite link with whoever sends in their receipts and invoices.

2. They tap the link, which opens a chat with the Otto Capture number, and send the pre-filled message to connect.

3. From then on, they send a photo or PDF straight into that client's [review queue](/capture/guides/reviewing/).

</Steps>

## Sharing the invite

Open the client's settings and go to **Client submission**. The **WhatsApp** area holds the client's invite link. The same link works for everyone, so you can pass it to more than one person. Copy the link on its own, or a ready-to-send message with the link in it.

You don't need to know or register anyone's phone number in advance.

## What the person sending documents sees

When they send the registration message, a reply confirms which client they're connected to. After that, each document they send is confirmed as received.

<ZoomableImage
  src={phoneRegistration}
  alt="A client's WhatsApp chat showing the registration message being sent and the reply confirming the connection"
  label="WhatsApp registration chat"
/>

If there's something you should know about a document, the sender can add a note when they send it, for example "client entertainment". It shows with the document when you review it.

WhatsApp takes the same [file types](/capture/getting-started/#supported-file-types) as any other route. Voice notes, videos, and shared locations aren't processed.

## One number, one client for now

A WhatsApp number can be connected to one client at a time. If someone connected to one client sends another client's code, they're asked to contact you. To move them across, remove their number from the first client, then they can register with the second client's code.

<Aside>
  Support for submitting to more than one business from the same number is on
  the way.
</Aside>

## Managing who's connected

The **WhatsApp** area lists everyone connected to the client under **Linked WhatsApp senders**.

- **Remove** disconnects a sender's number. They can't submit until they register again.
- **Rotate link** creates a new invite link and code. Anyone who hasn't connected yet can no longer join with the old one, and people already connected stay connected.

<Aside type="caution" title="Removing someone for good">
  Removing a sender doesn't invalidate the code. If they still have the current
  invite link or code, they can reconnect. To keep someone out for good, remove
  them and then rotate the link.
</Aside>

Only admins can rotate the link and remove senders. Anyone in your organisation can view and share the invite.
