Each client in Capture represents a single accounting platform organisation. After connecting your accounting platform, you can configure how documents are processed and published for that client.

## Email prefix

Each client has its own email address for receiving documents. You set its prefix in **General**, and the address follows the format:

```
{prefix}@{your-organisation}.ottocapture.email
```

For example, if your organisation is "smith-accounting" and you set the prefix to "acme", the address is `acme@smith-accounting.ottocapture.email`. With a [custom email domain](/capture/guides/custom-email-domain/), `{prefix}@{your-custom-domain}` works as well.

The prefix is also part of the client's [submission page](/capture/getting-started/client-submission-page/) address. Changing it doesn't break a link you've already shared: the page redirects to the new address. The **Client submission** tab shows the finished addresses.

## Default publish state

On Xero, you choose the state Otto creates new bills and sales invoices in when you complete setup for the client: **Draft**, **Awaiting approval**, or **Awaiting payment** (see [Xero publish states](/capture/platforms/xero/#publish-states)). You can change it later on the client's **Accounting platform** tab, and override it for a single document during review. If you're not sure, start with **Draft**: it gives you the most control before anything is sent or paid.

<Aside type="caution" title="Please note">
  FreeAgent and QuickBooks Online have no publish state to set. On FreeAgent,
  bills enter the ledger as soon as they're published, and sales invoices are
  created as Draft. See publishing to
  [FreeAgent](/capture/platforms/freeagent/#publishing) and [QuickBooks
  Online](/capture/platforms/quickbooks/#publishing).
</Aside>

## Synced accounting data

Capture syncs the client's accounts, tax rates, currencies, and tracking categories (classes on QuickBooks Online), and Otto uses them when he suggests the coding. On a bill from a supplier whose bills you've published for this client before, he also draws on how you coded them. See what syncs from [Xero](/capture/platforms/xero/#synced-data), [FreeAgent](/capture/platforms/freeagent/#synced-data), and [QuickBooks Online](/capture/platforms/quickbooks/#synced-data).

<ZoomableImage
  src={accountingPlatformSettings}

  label="Accounting platform tab"

During review, the account and tax rate lists show only those that fit the document. Bills show expense, asset, liability, and equity accounts (admin expenses and cost of sales categories on FreeAgent), and sales invoices show revenue accounts.

If you've changed something in the accounting platform, such as adding an account, click **Refresh all** on the client's **Accounting platform** tab, or refresh one kind of data on its own.
