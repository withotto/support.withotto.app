Otto reads your clients' receipts, bills, and invoices and extracts their data. You review what he found and publish it to the client's accounting platform.

## Creating your account

You can sign up for Otto Capture directly. If you use Xero, you can also install Capture from the Xero App Store, which signs you in with your Xero login and prefills what it can (see [Xero](/capture/platforms/xero/)). During sign-up you name your organisation and choose the email address your clients will use to send in documents.

When you sign in, Otto shows you what to do next: a short setup checklist at first, then the clients with documents waiting on you.

## Setup steps

<Steps>
    1. **Connect your accounting platform:** Authorise Capture to access your client's Xero, FreeAgent, or QuickBooks Online data. See [Connecting your accounting platform](/capture/platforms/connecting-your-accounting-platform/).

    2. **Configure your client:** Set an email prefix for email submissions and, on Xero, a default publish state. See [Configuring clients](/capture/getting-started/configuring-clients/).

    3. **Submit documents:** Upload files, forward emails, or share a WhatsApp invite or submission page link with the people who hold the receipts. See [Submitting documents](/capture/getting-started/submitting-documents/).

    4. **Review and publish:** Check what Otto extracted, correct anything he got wrong, and publish. See [Reviewing and publishing](/capture/guides/reviewing/).

</Steps>

## Supported file types

| Category         | Formats                                           |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Documents        | PDF                                               |
| Images           | JPEG, PNG, HEIC, HEIF, WebP, TIFF, GIF, BMP, AVIF |
| Office documents | DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, ODT, ODS                    |
| Web pages        | HTML                                              |

Each file you upload, or a client sends by WhatsApp or through one of their pages, can be up to 25 MB. Otto reads documents of up to 50 pages, so split anything longer into smaller files.

<Aside type="tip">
  For best results, submit clear, high-resolution images or native PDFs rather
  than scanned documents.
</Aside>
