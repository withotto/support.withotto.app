Otto Capture cuts the manual data entry out of bookkeeping. Otto reads each receipt, bill, and invoice, extracts the detail, and suggests the coding from how that client has been coded before. You review, and publish to your accounting platform.

## How it works

1. **Submit:** Upload documents through the web interface, forward them by email, or let clients send them over WhatsApp or their own client submission page
2. **Extract:** Otto reads each document and extracts supplier details, amounts, line items, and tax information
3. **Review:** Check the extracted data and make any corrections
4. **Publish:** Publish the bill or invoice to your accounting platform, with the original document attached where your platform supports it

## Supported document types

- **Bills** (accounts payable): supplier invoices, utility bills, receipts
- **Purchase credit notes** (accounts payable): credits from a supplier
- **Sales invoices** (accounts receivable): customer invoices you raise
- **Sales credit notes** (accounts receivable): credits you issue to a customer

On QuickBooks Online, Otto publishes bills and purchase credit notes only. See [QuickBooks Online](/capture/platforms/quickbooks#publishing).

## Missing receipts

For Xero clients, Capture can also help you chase the receipts you don't have yet. You import the client's unreconciled bank transactions, choose which ones to ask about, and share a page where the client answers each payment with a receipt, a note, or both. See [Missing receipts](/capture/missing-receipts).
