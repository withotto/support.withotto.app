## Connecting and disconnecting

### How do I disconnect a client?

In the client settings, click **Disconnect**. Capture shows what will be deleted and what stays before you confirm.

### What happens to my data if I disconnect?

- Documents already in Capture stay in Capture, whether published or not
- Bills and invoices already published stay in your accounting platform
- The data synced from the platform (contacts, accounts, tax rates, currencies, and tracking categories or classes) is deleted from Capture
- The supplier matches Otto has learned for the client are deleted, and reconnecting doesn't bring them back
- Otto stops using bills published before you disconnected to suggest coding, and reconnecting doesn't change that
- Bank transactions you imported for [Missing receipts](/capture/missing-receipts/) are kept, but the client's missing receipts link stops working until you reconnect
- You can reconnect at any time to sync the data again and resume publishing

See [Disconnecting and reconnecting](/capture/platforms/connecting-your-accounting-platform/#disconnecting-and-reconnecting) for when synced data is kept instead.

### Can I reconnect after disconnecting?

Yes. Open the client settings and click **Reconnect**. Capture syncs the platform's data again and Otto resumes publishing.

### A client shows "Disconnected" or "Connection failed". What happened?

Capture can no longer use the connection to the client's organisation. Usually someone revoked access inside the platform, or, for a Xero client, nothing was published or refreshed for 97 days (see [When Capture ends a Xero connection](/capture/platforms/connecting-your-accounting-platform/#when-capture-ends-a-xero-connection)). Organisation admins get a notification, and you fix it by opening the client settings and clicking **Reconnect**.

## Data sync

### What data does Capture sync from my accounting platform?

Contacts, account codes or categories, tax rates, currencies, tracking categories (Xero) or classes (QuickBooks Online), and basic organisation details. See [Data flow](/capture/guides/data-flow/) for the full list.

### How often does data sync?

A full sync runs when you first connect a client. After that, you refresh it yourself whenever something changes in the platform.

### I added a new account or category, but it's not showing in Capture

Click **Refresh** beside accounts on the client's **Accounting platform** tab. The new account is available once the refresh completes.

### Does Capture modify anything in my accounting platform?

Capture only **creates** new bills, invoices, credit notes, contacts, and attachments, and on Xero and QuickBooks Online adds a currency if you choose to during review. It never modifies or deletes existing data. See [what Capture does not modify](/capture/guides/data-flow/#what-capture-does-not-modify).

## Submitting documents

### How can client documents be submitted?

You upload files yourself, or the client emails them to their own address, sends them over [WhatsApp](/capture/getting-started/whatsapp-submission/), or uses their [client submission page](/capture/getting-started/client-submission-page/). Xero and FreeAgent clients can also send them in answer to your questions on their [client missing receipts page](/capture/missing-receipts/client-page/). Everything arrives in the same review queue.

### Do the people sending documents need an Otto Capture account?

No. WhatsApp submission, the client submission page, and the client missing receipts page all work without an account, a download, or a password. You share a link, and that's all anyone needs.

### Can I stop a submission link working?

Yes. Admins can do it at any time, and documents already submitted aren't affected.

- **WhatsApp:** remove the sender, then rotate the invite link, or they can reconnect with the old one. See [Managing who's connected](/capture/getting-started/whatsapp-submission/#managing-whos-connected).
- **Client submission page:** rotating the link stops it working for everyone holding it. You can also turn the page off. See [Rotating and turning the page off](/capture/getting-started/client-submission-page/#rotating-and-turning-the-page-off).

### A client says their submission link isn't working

Usually the link has been rotated or the page turned off, in which case share the current one from the client's **Client submission** settings. A link also stops working while the client is disconnected from its accounting platform, and every submission page link changes if you change your organisation prefix.

## Missing receipts

### Which clients can I use Missing receipts for?

Clients connected to Xero or FreeAgent. For a Xero client, you upload Xero's Uncoded statement lines export. For a FreeAgent client, Capture imports the unexplained transactions straight from FreeAgent. Capture doesn't import bank transactions for QuickBooks Online clients. See [Missing receipts](/capture/missing-receipts/).

### Does Capture read my client's bank transactions from Xero?

No. You export the Uncoded statement lines report from Xero and upload the file, and that file is the only way transactions reach Capture. See [Importing from Xero](/capture/missing-receipts/xero/).

### Does Capture read my client's bank transactions from FreeAgent?

Yes, when you click **Fetch transactions** on the client's **Missing receipts** tab, and not otherwise. Capture reads the transactions FreeAgent hasn't explained yet in the date range you choose, and changes nothing in FreeAgent. For a client connected as a single company, the FreeAgent user who authorised the connection needs **Banking** access or higher. See [Importing from FreeAgent](/capture/missing-receipts/freeagent/).

### Anyone with a missing receipts link can see the client's transactions. Is that safe?

The link shows the date, amount, description, and bank account of each transaction on the client's current list, to anyone holding it, with no login. Share it only with the people who need it, and exclude anything the client doesn't need to be asked about. Admins can rotate the link or turn the page off at any time. See [What anyone with the link can see](/capture/missing-receipts/client-page/#what-anyone-with-the-link-can-see).

## Processing and extraction

### How long does processing take?

Most documents are ready within a minute, and nearly all within a few minutes.

### What document types can Capture process?

Bills and purchase credit notes, and sales invoices and sales credit notes. Otto works out the type himself. On QuickBooks Online, Capture publishes only bills and supplier credits (see [QuickBooks Online](/capture/platforms/quickbooks/#publishing)).

### What file formats are supported?

PDF, common image formats (JPEG, PNG, HEIC, HEIF, WebP, TIFF, GIF, BMP, AVIF), Office documents (DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, ODT, ODS), and HTML.

### Is there a size or page limit?

Each file you upload in Capture, or a client sends by WhatsApp or through their submission or missing receipts page, can be up to 25 MB. Otto reads documents of up to 50 pages, so split anything longer into smaller files and upload those. See [Supported file types](/capture/getting-started/#supported-file-types).

### What if Otto extracts the wrong data?

Otto doesn't always get it right, especially with unfamiliar suppliers. Every field is editable during review, so you can put things straight before publishing.

### What if Otto can't determine the document type?

The document is marked **Needs classification**. Set the type on the review screen, and Otto carries on from there.

### What if there are multiple receipts in one image?

Capture marks the file **Needs manual split**. If they're separate documents, split the file and send each one. If the pages all belong to one bill, such as an invoice with the supplier's own expenses attached, click **Process as one document** on the review screen. See [How Capture processes documents](/capture/guides/processing/#1-triage).

### A document is in a currency my client's platform doesn't support

Convert the amounts during review. On Xero and QuickBooks Online, you may be able to add the currency to the organisation instead. See [Currency and conversion](/capture/guides/reviewing/#currency-and-conversion).

### Why can't I add a currency to a Xero client?

Multi-currency isn't part of every Xero plan, and Otto only offers to add a currency where the organisation can use more than one. Otherwise, convert the amounts before publishing. See [Xero currencies](/capture/platforms/xero/#currencies).

### Can I record a foreign currency document in my client's base currency?

Yes. Open the converter on the review screen and enter the total your client was actually charged in the base currency, and Otto works out the rate from it. Recording at the rate actually charged means the document agrees with the bank line. You can restore the original amounts at any point before publishing.

### The converter says my total is above (or below) the market rate. What does that mean?

It's a check for a mistyped total, not a judgement on it. Card fees and a gap between the issue and payment dates can both move the rate. If the total matches the statement, click **Apply anyway**. See [Checking the total against the market rate](/capture/guides/reviewing/#checking-the-total-against-the-market-rate).

## Multi-organisation

### Can one account manage multiple organisations?

Yes. Each accounting platform organisation is represented as a separate client in Capture. You can have as many clients as you need within your practice.

### Do clients share any data?

No. Each client's documents, contacts, and accounting data are kept separate.

## Security

### How are documents stored?

Documents are stored in encrypted cloud storage (Amazon S3). Each file is organised by organisation and client, with access controlled through signed URLs that expire after a short period.

### Where does Otto read my documents, and are they used to train AI?

Otto reads your documents with third-party LLMs hosted in the UK or the EU. The AI providers we use must not use what we send to train any model, and must not log, store or review it. Every model has to meet a strict set of rules before Capture uses it. The [privacy policy](https://withotto.app/privacy-policy/#how-we-process-your-documents) sets them out in full.

Documents your clients send by WhatsApp pass through Meta first, which may hold them outside the UK and the EU.

### Are documents scanned for malware?

Yes. Every uploaded document is scanned for malware before processing. If a threat is detected, the document is quarantined and won't be processed.

### How are accounting platform credentials stored?

Access tokens are encrypted at rest using symmetric encryption with support for key rotation. Capture never stores your accounting platform username or password.

### Anyone with a submission link can send documents. Is that safe?

Sending a document is all it allows: there's no way to see anything already in Capture or reach any other client, and nothing reaches your accounting platform until someone in your organisation has reviewed it. Every file is scanned for malware, and there's a limit to stop a link flooding your queue. Treat the link like a key, and rotate it if it leaks.

### Who can access my data?

In Capture, only users in your organisation can access your documents and client data. Your data is kept completely separate from other organisations, and we enforce this separation at multiple layers, from the application through to the database, to prevent data from being accidentally exposed to the wrong organisation.

## Errors and troubleshooting

### A document failed to publish. What should I do?

Check the error message on the document. Most often, an account code or tax rate has changed in the accounting platform since Capture last synced it: refresh the client's synced data, update the line, then publish again. If the client shows **Connection failed**, reconnect it first.

### A document is stuck in "Processing"

Documents occasionally take longer to process if the queue is busy. If a document has been processing for more than 15 minutes, please contact support.

### Why was my document quarantined?

The malware scan found a potential threat, so the file won't be processed. Let the sender know so they can scan their device, then send the document again. If you think the scan got it wrong, please contact support.

### How does Otto Capture pricing work?

Capture is priced per client, per month, across three tiers. You don't choose a plan: each client sits automatically on whichever tier is cheapest for the number of documents it published that month, so you never overpay or move clients between tiers to find the best deal.

| Tier   | Monthly price | Included documents |
| ------ | ------------- | ------------------ |
| Small  | £9            | 50                 |
| Medium | £15           | 125                |
| Large  | £29           | 350                |

All prices exclude VAT. You receive one monthly bill covering every connected client, each charged on its own cheapest tier.

### What sets a client's tier each month?

A client's tier is set by how many documents are published to your accounting platform that month. Published documents are what's counted, and Capture always lands the client on the cheapest tier for that volume. A document published as a draft in Xero still counts, since it has been published. Documents that are never published, including any you discard, don't add to the count.

### Are discarded documents charged?

Discarding a document doesn't publish it, so it doesn't add to a client's published volume or move the client to a higher tier. The same is true of documents that fail during processing or are quarantined by the malware scan.

### Are credit notes charged?

A credit note you publish counts as one published document, the same as a bill or invoice. Credit notes you discard or never publish aren't charged.

### Is a client charged if nothing is published?

A client with no documents published in a month is not charged. Billing follows what you publish to your accounting platform, so a connected client you publish nothing for costs you nothing that month.

### What happens if a client publishes more than its tier's included documents?

There's no price cliff. Each document over a tier's included number is charged individually at that tier's per-document rate, until the next tier up becomes the cheaper option, and from then on the client sits on that tier. Either way the client lands on whichever is cheapest for its volume, so rising usage is smooth rather than a sudden jump between prices.

### How am I billed, and is there a minimum commitment?

You're billed monthly in arrears for actual usage, in a single bill covering all your connected clients. There's no minimum term and no minimum number of clients, and you can stop at any time. If you do, you only pay for the usage in your final billing period.
