Missing receipts in Otto Capture puts the payments you have questions about on one page, where the client answers each one with a receipt, a note, or both. A note stays with the receipt it relates to, so it's there when you review the document. It's available for clients connected to Xero or FreeAgent.

## How it works

<Steps>

1. An admin turns on the client's missing receipts page, which adds a **Missing receipts** tab to that client.

2. You import the client's unreconciled bank transactions into Capture: from a file you export from Xero, or straight from FreeAgent.

3. You prepare the client's list: exclude anything the client doesn't need to explain, and create the list from the rest.

4. You share the client's link. The client opens it, sends a file or writes a note against each payment, and you see their answers as they arrive. Files they send reach the client's review queue like any other document.

</Steps>

## Turning it on for a client

An admin opens the client's settings, goes to **Client submission**, and clicks **Turn on client missing receipts page**. That creates the client's link and adds the **Missing receipts** tab to the client. While the page is off, there's no tab. See [Client missing receipts page](/capture/missing-receipts/client-page/) for sharing, rotating, and turning off the link.

## Importing bank transactions

How the transactions reach Capture depends on the client's accounting platform:

| Platform      | How transactions arrive                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Xero**      | You export Xero's **Uncoded statement lines** report as a CSV and upload it. See [Importing from Xero](/capture/missing-receipts/xero/)                                                                |
| **FreeAgent** | You choose a date range and click **Fetch transactions**, and Capture reads the unexplained transactions straight from FreeAgent. See [Importing from FreeAgent](/capture/missing-receipts/freeagent/) |

Either way, the client isn't asked about anything until you put it on their list.

### Importing again

Import again whenever you want to bring the list up to date. Each import replaces the transactions you prepare the list from. A transaction that has dropped out has usually been reconciled, so it comes off the client's list when you next update it. Check the newest import covers the dates you meant to: when you update the list, Capture shows the dates of anything about to come off.

Anything you've excluded stays excluded, and anything the client has answered keeps its answer.

## Preparing the client's list

Click **Prepare list** to choose what the client is asked about. Every transaction in the newest import is included unless you exclude it, so the job here is taking out what the client doesn't need to explain, such as salaries or transfers between their own accounts.

<ZoomableImage
  src={prepareList}
  alt="The Prepare the client's list screen, with filters across the top, a table of transactions each marked On the client's list or Excluded, three selected, and a bar offering Exclude 2, 2 shown to your client now and Ask about 1"
  label="Prepare the client's list screen"
/>

Tick transactions and click **Exclude**, using the filters to find them. When the rest is right, click **Create client's list**, or **Update client's list** if the client already has one, and confirm.

<ZoomableImage
  src={updateListDialog}
  alt="The Update this client's list dialog, saying 11 transactions will be on this client's list and that you've excluded one, above a panel headed What your client will be able to see, with Cancel and Update list buttons"
  label="Update list dialog"
/>

Creating or updating the list puts those transactions on the client's page straight away. Capture doesn't notify the client, so let them know yourself when there's something new to answer.

<Aside type="caution">
  Adding transactions waits until you update the list, but excluding doesn't. If
  you exclude a transaction that's already on the client's current list, it
  comes off the client's page immediately, and any file they sent for it moves
  to **Not linked to a payment**. The message after you exclude says how many
  were on the list.
</Aside>

A client has one current list. Updating it replaces the previous one, and a transaction the client answered keeps its answer if it's still on the new list.

## Seeing the client's answers

On the **Missing receipts** tab, click **See answers** to see each payment with the client's note and files, which payments are still unanswered, and any files **Not linked to a payment**. A payment counts as answered once it has a note or a file.

<ZoomableImage
  src={answers}
  alt="The client's answers page, listing payments under Money out and Money in, each answered one showing the client's note and the file they sent with its status and an Ask again button, and a Not linked to a payment section at the foot"
  label="Client's answers page"
/>

A note the client writes against a payment appears on the document's review screen as **Note from sender**, so whoever reviews it can check the coding against what the client said.

### Correcting an answer

To put a payment back in front of the client, use **Remove from this payment** to take one file off, for example when the client sent the wrong receipt, or **Ask again** to clear the whole answer.

<ZoomableImage
  src={askAgainConfirm}
  alt="A payment on the answers page after Ask again has been clicked, with the button replaced by Confirm?, among other payments still showing their Ask again buttons"
  label="Ask again confirmation"
/>

Neither deletes anything: the files stay in Capture and move to **Not linked to a payment** on the client's page. The client isn't told, so let them know.

## Billing

Files sent from the client's page are documents like any other, and count towards the client's tier only when you publish them. Importing transactions and preparing lists are not charged. See [Billing](/capture/faq/#billing).

## What Missing receipts does not do

- It doesn't import bank transactions for QuickBooks Online clients.
- It doesn't import transactions on a schedule. You upload a Xero export, or click **Fetch transactions** for FreeAgent.
- It doesn't reconcile anything or change anything in the client's accounting platform.
- It doesn't contact the client. You share the link and tell them when there's something to answer.
