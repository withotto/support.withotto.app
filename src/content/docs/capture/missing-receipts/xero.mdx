[Missing receipts](/capture/missing-receipts/) in Otto Capture starts from Xero's **Uncoded statement lines** report, which lists the statement lines that haven't been reconciled yet. You export it as a CSV and upload it on the client's **Missing receipts** tab. Capture doesn't read bank transactions through the Xero connection, and needs no extra access in Xero for this.

## Exporting from Xero

<Steps>

1. On the client's **Missing receipts** tab, click **Open Uncoded statement lines in Xero**. It opens the report for this client's organisation, with every bank account and the last six months selected.

   If the link doesn't open the report, go to **Accounting**, then **Bank accounts** in Xero, open the ⋮ menu, and choose **Uncoded statement lines**. Opened that way, the report keeps its last filters, so check it covers every account.

2. Check that the bank accounts and the date range cover the payments you're chasing.

3. Choose **Export**, then **CSV**.

4. Back in Capture, choose the file or drop it on the box, and click **Check file**. Upload it without opening or editing it first: a spreadsheet program can change the dates and amounts when it saves.

</Steps>

If you can't see **Uncoded statement lines** in Xero, check your access to that organisation in Xero.

## Checking the import

Capture checks the file and shows you what it found before anything is stored.

<ZoomableImage
  src={importPreview}
  alt="The Check this import screen, listing the newest transaction as 14 September 2026 (yesterday), a date range, 4 of 6 transactions to import, and two bank accounts: Business Current Account marked Recognised, and Deposit Account marked Not imported: no matching bank account in Xero"
  label="Check this import screen"
/>

Nothing inside the file says which organisation it came from, so the bank accounts are how you tell it's the right client's file. An account Capture can't match to one of the client's bank accounts in Xero isn't imported.

Click **Store transactions** to keep them, then [prepare the client's list](/capture/missing-receipts/#preparing-the-clients-list).

## Importing again

Import a fresh export whenever you want to bring the list up to date. Each import replaces the transactions you prepare the list from, and answers and exclusions carry over: see [Importing again](/capture/missing-receipts/#importing-again).

If Capture can't read a file, export it again from Xero and upload it without opening it.
